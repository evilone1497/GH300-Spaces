# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation describes how OctoAcme takes work from an initial idea through delivery and learning. Projects begin with initiation and approval, move into planning and backlog creation, and proceed through iterative execution and tracking. Teams then release and deploy changes, and close out by reflecting on results and identifying continuous improvements.

Throughout the lifecycle, OctoAcme emphasizes clear ownership, customer outcomes, risk and dependency management, quality practices, and regular stakeholder communication. The overview below provides a starting point; use the linked guides for details, checklists, and templates.

## Process Documents

- [Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md) — Validate a project idea, align stakeholders, define success criteria, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
- [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) — Turn an approved initiative into a prioritized backlog, release plan, and delivery approach.
- [Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — Coordinate day-to-day delivery, project-board workflow, quality checks, progress reporting, and blocker escalation.
- [Risk Management & Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — Maintain and monitor risks and dependencies, communicate status, and use escalation paths.
- [Release & Deployment Guide](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — Prepare, deploy, verify, and, when needed, roll back releases.
- [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — Capture learnings after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents and track improvement actions.
- [Roles & Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) — Responsibilities, goals, and communication patterns for developers, product managers, and project managers.
